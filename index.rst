Welcome to MetaModels!
======================

This is the official documentation of `MetaModels <https://now.metamodel.me>`_, an extension
for the `Contao Content Management System <https://contao.org>`_. The MetaModels extension makes
it possible to store structured data in the Contao CMS and display it on the website - more
about the structure and usage can be found under :ref:`introdution_was-ist-metamodels`. The common
abbreviation for MetaModels is 'MM'.


This documentation is divided into three sections:

    :ref:`index_manual` - General documentation about MetaModels.

    :ref:`index_cookbook` - Workflows and 'best practice' examples for working with MetaModels.

    :ref:`index_reference` - Documentation about events, interfaces, and much more.


Support and Donations
---------------------
The MetaModels extension is a free extension based on open source and needs the support of an
active community for continuous further development. Contributions in the form of programming or
reporting bugs as well as own workflows are welcome. Information can be found on the
`MetaModels project website <https://now.metamodel.me>`_ on `Github
<https://github.com/MetaModels>`_ or in the `Contao forum <https://community.contao.org/de/forumdisplay.php?149-MetaModels>`_.

Financial contributions in the form of donations are also important for the continuous further
development and ongoing maintenance of the project. More information on the
`MetaModels donation page <https://now.metamodel.me/de/unterstuetzer/spenden>`_.
