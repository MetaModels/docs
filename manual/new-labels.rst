New Labels and Icons for MetaModels
=====================================

.. warning:: In December 2015, the labels and icons of MetaModels were revised and unified,
   and new icons were introduced. As a result, there may be differences between screenshots
   and the current backend.

In the MM call of November 2015, it was decided to update the labels and icons used in the backend.
The goal is to achieve a consistent "nomenclature" for MetaModels elements and to make it easier
to associate a label/icon with its content.

As an appendix, there is a `diagram <http://metamodels.readthedocs.org/de/latest/_images/neue-bezeichnung-uebersicht_de_v2.jpg>`_
comparing the old version with the new version.

The labels and icons are being replaced gradually with the respective updates to the MetaModels
components — as well as in this manual.
